# The Daylight Moon — Audio Reading Outline

**Midnight Sun · Natal reading · June 9, 1989 · 1:26 PM PDT · Stayton, Oregon**
Running time: about 55–65 minutes · Follows the PDF section by section, so the client can read along or come back to it later.

---

## How to use this outline

- **Say** lines are written to be read aloud close to word for word. They hold the image or the turn, the part that is hardest to improvise.
- **Points** are talking points. Speak them in your own words and follow the client's chart wherever it takes you.
- **Pause** marks a deliberate silence of two or three seconds. On audio, silence does the work that white space does on the page.
- Read the jargon, then translate it straight away into plain language. Don't leave a technical term hanging for more than one sentence.
- Keep everything in the present tense. When you reach timing, describe weather. Don't name events.

## 0. Cold open (1 min)

**Say:** "June, a quarter hour past the Sun's highest point, the Willamette Valley green to its edges. The light has stopped climbing and begun, almost imperceptibly, to lean west. In the east, over the Cascade foothills, a thin Moon hangs in full daylight, easy to mistake for cloud. You arrive between them."

*Pause.* Then the brand ident or music sting, if you use one.

## 1. Orientation (3–4 min)

**Points**

- What a birth chart is: the sky drawn from one place at one minute. Stayton, 1:26 on a Friday afternoon.
- Three words: planet (the drive), sign (its climate), house (the room of life where it lives). Aspects are planets in conversation.
- How to hold it: terrain and inclination, not a verdict. Choice matters, and circumstance limits choice.
- Method, kept brief: tropical zodiac, whole sign houses, Placidus noted in the PDF. Day chart. Explain sect in one breath: the old astrologers read day and night births differently, and in a day chart the Sun and Jupiter lead.

**Say:** "The chart deserves careful reading, and so do you."

## 2. The shape of the sky (3 min)

**Points**

- The big three: Gemini Sun, Virgo Moon, Virgo rising.
- **Mercury runs almost everything.** It rules the rising sign, the Midheaven, the Sun, the Moon and Jupiter. Few charts lean this hard on one planet.
- The closed circle: Mercury lives in Venus's sign, Venus lives in the Moon's sign, and the Moon lives in Mercury's. No one planet gets the final word.
- **No fire.** No planet sits in Aries, Leo or Sagittarius. Plant this early and come back to it in section 12.
- Born on Venus's day (Friday), in the hour of the Sun.

## 3. The Rising: Virgo, 23°49′ (4 min)

**Image:** Agrippa's figure for this decan, a white-haired old man stooping on a staff, wrapped in a mantle.

**Points**

- The harvest maiden sorting grain. The rising sign shows up in the body and the manner first.
- Reading the room: people mistake Virgo's attention for coolness.
- The bounds of Mars put an edge on Virgo's precision, and the blade turns inward. Self-audit gets mistaken for honesty.
- The Moon rose about two hours before this degree, so feeling shows on the face first.

**Noticing:** the first correction made each morning, and whose voice it speaks in.

**Close, Say:** "The staff is for leaning on. It also tests the ground ahead."

## 4. The Chart Ruler: Mercury in Taurus, 9th, 28°55′ (5 min)

**Image:** Mercury walks backward out of Gemini in May 1989, stops on June 5, and is barely moving at birth, beside the Pleiades.

**Points**

- The ruler "had to go back for something before it could go forward."
- Taurus thinks with the hands. The 9th house is belief, teachers and long journeys, and here belief has to be something you can hold.
- Slowness gives way to accuracy. The second thought is the true one.
- The Pleiades: Ptolemy gave them the nature of the Moon and Mars, and later tradition ties them to the eyes. Weeping and seeing. A mind at its clearest near grief.
- Mercury square the Nodes: the messenger standing at the crossroads.

**Noticing:** facts or textures when explaining something loved.

**Close, Say:** "Mercury crossed back into Gemini three days after your birth. You were born in the doorway, one foot still on the Bull's back."

## 5. The Sun: Gemini, 10th, 18°57′ (5 min)

**Image:** Twelve days before the solstice, the Sun 68° high and crossing the meridian at 1:10. Orion stands behind it, with the Midheaven on Mintaka in Orion's Belt.

**Points**

- Thinking in pairs. Agrippa's decan image: the piper and the digger, music and labor in one field.
- The 10th house wants witness, a sense that the work lands and gets used.
- Range is the gift, and the same range is the wound: the fear of never having chosen one thing properly.
- Mars rules the decan and the bounds, which gives sharp wit and makes argument both a tool and a trap.
- Hand off to Jupiter.

**Noticing:** the interests called "little."

**Close, Say:** "Stand where people can see you. Notice what you are hunting."

## 6. The Moon: Virgo, 1st, 3°01′ (5 min)

**Image:** A daylight Moon, chalk on a blue board, risen at 11:33 AM. Agrippa's figures: a good maid and a man casting seed. *This section gives the reading its title, so slow down here.*

**Points**

- Comfort through order. The body keeps a ledger, and distress runs through the gut, the skin and the hands.
- Practical early care, love as tasks done. "Why being useful never quite felt like being held."
- 1st or 12th house depending on the system: the face shows feeling first, and solitude works like a nutrient.
- Trine Uranus: fast inner weather and comfort in the unconventional. Square Mercury: a feeling analyzed until nothing is left of it.
- Crescent phase: new light pushing against the dark.

**Noticing:** what the hands reach for when upset.

**Close, Say:** "Seed cast in a field. Some of it is for eating. Some of it is for next year."

## 7. Jupiter, the hidden benefic (4 min)

**Image:** The brightest lamp in the night sky goes unseen. It met the Sun about eleven hours before birth, in the dark before dawn.

**Points**

- Combust versus cazimi. Explain both terms. Jupiter sits 20′ from the Sun, just outside the 17′ limit: "a breath outside the throne room, close enough to hear what is said inside."
- In detriment in Gemini: a hundred fragments in place of one meaning. Generosity through information and introductions.
- The benefic of sect, fused with the Sun at the top of the chart. Luck comes through work, and the client is the last to see it.
- Jupiter also rules home (4th) and partners (7th).

**Noticing:** ask three people what you are lucky in, and write the answers down before arguing.

**Close, Say:** "A lamp carried at noon still burns. Look at where you set it down."

## 8. Venus in Cancer, 11th, conjunct Chiron (5 min)

**Image:** Agrippa's crowned young woman in fine clothes. Across the sky sits 1989's heavy weather: Saturn, Uranus and Neptune together in Capricorn.

**Points**

- Love by sheltering: the hearth, the pantry, the chair kept for one person.
- The opposition: Saturn brings distance and duty, Uranus the sudden exit, Neptune the fog. Love collides with history. "The person you love lives on the other side of a wall you did not build."
- Chiron half a degree away: the healer's wound sits exactly where love lives. Watch for a love that only knows how to nurse.
- The 11th house: friendship and chosen family. Venus hosts the chart ruler, so the mind rents its room from the heart.
- Mention that Venus is lord of the current year and that you'll come back to it.

**Noticing:** the two lists, who you feed and who feeds you.

**Close, Say:** "The crown is a fine thing. It is also heavy. Ask who placed it on her head."

## 9. Mars in Cancer, 11th, in fall (4 min)

**Image:** Fire underwater. A soldier inside a shell, and a crab's claw that closes slowly.

**Points**

- Fall, explained plainly: working against the grain. That produces adaptations, some ingenious and some costly.
- Anger shows up first as protectiveness on someone else's behalf. On the client's own behalf it becomes indirection: silence, pointed kindness, a door shut a little hard.
- Contrary to sect, Mars is the planet out of step with the chart, and it carries nearly all the chart's heat.
- The 11th house: the client fights for their people and is wounded hardest by them. The sextile to the rising sign is the good news, because this Mars can be put to work.

**Noticing:** where swallowed anger lands in the body. Hand there.

**Close, Say:** "The crab's claw closes slowly. Decide what is worth holding."

## 10. Saturn and the dissolving wall (5 min)

**Image:** Agrippa's figures: two women, and a man looking up at a bird in flight. Asking for things that cannot be done, searching for things that cannot be known.

**Points**

- **The history beat:** Saturn and Neptune met three times in 1989. The Berlin Wall opened on November 9, four days before their last exact meeting. The client's Saturn sits on that degree.
- A generation born as solid structures turned out to be sand. The instinct to build and the instinct to doubt run together.
- Saturn is strong here, in its own sign and in sect, so it can be trusted with weight. In the 5th house, play is serious, creativity matures late, and questions about children carry weight. Speak carefully here and make no predictions.
- Neptune adds a dream that keeps checking whether it has earned the right to exist.
- The brand's Saturn question: a promise made by someone much younger, still being kept.

**Noticing:** what the client only enjoys once the work is done, and whether the work is ever done.

**Close, Say:** "The bird is still flying. Nobody has caught it, and nobody has stopped looking up."

## 11. Pluto in Scorpio, 3rd (3 min)

**Points**

- Pluto at perihelion in September 1989, its closest point to the Sun in 248 years. A decade fluent in secrets.
- The 3rd house: words weigh a lot, the client knows which sentence would wound, and their curiosity runs toward archives and family stories.
- Siblings and early friends carry a charge: loyalty and rivalry, a shared secret.
- The sextile to Saturn and Neptune: obsession becomes craft.

**Noticing:** the unsaid sentence to a sibling or oldest friend, and whether the handwriting changes.

## 12. The Other Shore: Pisces 7th (3 min)

**Points**

- Two fish, one cord. Partners carry what Virgo can't: mess, faith, drift.
- How much order love can survive, and how much disorder.
- Jupiter rules partners and sits fused with the Sun at the top of the chart, so partners and vocation are knotted together. Combust: someone disappears in someone else's light.
- Neptune, with Saturn in the house of romance, is the modern ruler: the dream, then the person who wakes up beside it.

**Close, Say:** "Two fish, one cord. Neither one is steering."

## 13. The Unlit Hearth: no fire (3 min)

**Image:** A well-built house in autumn, the fireplace swept and never lit.

**Points**

- A missing element shows up as hunger as often as lack. The client looks for fire in partners, sudden risks and stages.
- Earth wants proof before it moves. Fire moves first.
- The two fire points: the South Node in Leo in the 12th (the performer who practices alone) and the Lot of Fortune in Sagittarius in the 4th (luck through home and land).
- Confidence comes last, after competence, and that can mean years spent waiting for permission no one was going to give.

**Close, Say:** "The hearth is swept. The wood is stacked. Strike the match."

## 14. The Nodes and the Lots (4 min)

**Points**

- The dragon image, the eclipse points. South Node in Leo in the 12th: the hidden performer. North Node in Aquarius in the 6th: bring the gift into the office, the kitchen, the Tuesday afternoon.
- Fortune in Sagittarius in the 4th: luck close to the ground. Spirit in Cancer in the 11th, beside Venus and Chiron: purpose through their people, with the wound and the vocation at the same address.
- Lilith in Libra in the 2nd: the agreeableness the client refuses to be paid in. Present this as a stranger, more modern point, and say so.

## 15. The Season: timing (10–12 min)

**Say first:** "A birth chart is fixed. The sky keeps moving. What follows is weather. Where the astrology can't support a specific outcome, this reading doesn't invent one."

1. **The Year of Venus** (June 2026 – June 2027). At age 37 the profection reaches the 2nd house: value, money, worth. *Noticing:* track every undercharge and overgive until midwinter.
2. **Nodal return** (exact October 14, 2026). The last one was January 2008, at age 18. The February 2026 solar eclipse landed on the North Node and squared Mercury. The February 20, 2027 lunar eclipse falls on the Moon. *Noticing:* read something written at eighteen as if a stranger wrote it.
3. **Saturn square Saturn** (June 1 and September 23, 2026; February 21, 2027). The client's Saturn return was in January 2019. Saturn in Aries is moving through the 8th house: debt, shared money, fear. **Say:** "Which commitments still belong to you, and which were made by a younger self who believed endurance and integrity were the same thing?"
4. **Uranus square the Moon** (June 18 and December 12, 2026; April 6, 2027). Changes at work press on home and the body. This is familiar lightning, only louder.
5. **Neptune square Venus and Chiron** (June 2027 – January 2029). A long fog over the hearth, but also an opening for compassion. Read the contract twice. Wait one full Moon cycle before acting on certainty.
6. **On the horizon:** Jupiter crosses the Moon (August 2027) and the rising sign (November 2027 – July 2028). Saturn reaches Mercury (May 2030). Uranus crosses the Sun, Jupiter and Midheaven (June 2030 – June 2031), the largest vocational turn in decades. The astrology can't say which direction it goes. Pluto squares Pluto in 2031–32.

**Say:** "What would you do with your range if you stopped apologizing for it?" *Pause.*

## 16. Closing (1 min)

**Say:** "The Sun has crossed the top of the sky and is leaning west. The Moon is still there in the east, thin, in daylight, easy to miss. Someone is standing in the valley, looking up, deciding which light to walk toward. Ask what it would take to see them both at once."

*Long pause.* Sign off. Don't summarize the reading.

## Optional: questions for a live session

Use these if the audio is recorded with the client present, or for a follow-up call.

- "Where in your life do you correct first and feel second?" (Rising and Moon)
- "What did you go back for before you could move forward?" (Mercury station)
- "Who do you feed, and who feeds you?" (Venus)
- "What promise from 2018 or 2019 are you still keeping?" (Saturn square)
- "What do you do brilliantly when no one is watching?" (South Node)

## Accuracy notes for you

- Calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris. 1:26 PM PDT is 20:26 UT. Virgo rising holds for roughly ±30 minutes, so if the client's birth time is uncertain, flag the house placements and the Moon's 1st/12th question.
- Whole sign and Placidus disagree for the Sun (10th/9th), Moon (1st/12th), Venus and Mars (11th/10th), Saturn, Uranus and Neptune (5th/4th), and Pluto (3rd/2nd). The PDF lists both.
- Jupiter is **not** cazimi. It is 20′ from the Sun, against the strict 17′ limit. Call it combust, or "a breath outside cazimi."
- Agrippa's decan images are paraphrased from *Three Books of Occult Philosophy*, so don't present them as direct quotes.
- Fixed-star longitudes are precessed to 1989. Mercury is about 55′ from Alcyone, and the MC is about 17′ from Mintaka.
